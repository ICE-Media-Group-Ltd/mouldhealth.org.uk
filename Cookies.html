Cookie Policy

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your settings and show the site owner how the site is used.

Cookies we use

Strictly necessary: these keep the website working, for example by protecting forms from spam. They do not need your consent.

Analytics and measurement: we use Google Tag Manager to load analytics and measurement tools. These may set cookies that record pages visited, time on site, the website you came from and general device information. This data is aggregated and used to improve the website.

Third-party cookies

Tags loaded through Google Tag Manager may be operated by third parties such as Google. Those providers set their own cookies and have their own privacy policies. We do not control them.

Your choices

Where required, non-essential cookies are only set after you have given consent. You can change or withdraw consent at any time through the cookie settings, if shown, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies. Blocking all cookies may stop parts of the website from working. Guidance is available at aboutcookies.org and ico.org.uk.

More information

For how we handle personal data, read our Privacy Policy. If you have a question about cookies, use the form on our Contact page.
